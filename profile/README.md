# Cyberpunk 2077 Phantom Liberty: Spy Thriller RPG Expansion

---

## What Is Cyberpunk 2077 Phantom Liberty?

Cyberpunk 2077 Phantom Liberty is a story driven RPG expansion developed by CD Projekt Red. It adds a spy thriller narrative to the world of Night City, introducing a new district, characters, missions, and choices that influence the course of the story.

The adventure centers on Dogtown, a dangerous district controlled by the powerful local leader Kurt Hansen. Players become involved in a high stakes operation involving NUSA President Rosalind Myers, sleeper agent Solomon Reed, and netrunner Songbird. Alliances, conflicting loyalties, and political intrigue shape the events that follow.

Phantom Liberty combines dialogue driven storytelling with exploration, combat, stealth, hacking, and character development. Missions can unfold in different ways depending on player decisions, abilities, and how individual encounters are handled.

The expansion is designed for Cyberpunk 2077 and builds on its established systems, including cyberware, weapons, character builds, and branching quests. Its central story adds a separate layer of intrigue while connecting with the wider narrative of the base game.

## Story and Setting

### Dogtown

Dogtown is a sealed district of Night City where armed factions, illicit businesses, and competing interests shape daily life. Its streets, markets, buildings, and restricted areas create a distinct setting for the expansion's missions.

The district rewards observation and careful navigation. Players can encounter side activities, discover hidden locations, and uncover details about the people who live under its unstable rule.

### A Political Crisis

The central narrative begins with a dangerous assignment involving NUSA President Rosalind Myers. The mission draws V into a conflict involving intelligence agencies, personal loyalties, and competing political interests.

As the story develops, information from different characters can change how events are understood. Trust becomes a recurring theme, and decisions may have lasting consequences for the people involved.

### Solomon Reed and Songbird

Solomon Reed is an experienced intelligence agent whose past and loyalties become important to the operation. Songbird is a skilled netrunner whose knowledge and personal objectives place her at the center of the crisis.

Their relationships with V form a major part of the narrative. Conversations, revelations, and difficult choices gradually expose the motives behind the operation.

## Gameplay Features

### Espionage Missions

Phantom Liberty places greater emphasis on covert operations, intelligence gathering, infiltration, and complicated relationships. Missions often require players to assess their surroundings, speak with key characters, and decide how to handle changing circumstances.

The spy thriller theme influences both the story and the structure of its assignments, creating a different narrative tone from many of the base game's activities.

### Combat and Stealth

Players can confront enemies directly or use stealth to avoid detection and control encounters. Available options depend on character attributes, equipped cyberware, weapons, and acquired abilities.

Different character builds support different tactics. A combat-focused character may rely on weapons and mobility, while a stealth-focused character can emphasize quiet movement, hacking, and careful target selection.

### Hacking and Cyberware

Cyberware remains central to character customization. Players can combine implants and abilities to suit their preferred style, whether that means direct combat, covert movement, quickhacking, or a mixture of several tactics.

The expansion also ties its encounters into the broader character-building systems of Cyberpunk 2077, allowing players to adapt their capabilities as they progress.

### Dialogue and Narrative Choices

Conversations are more than background storytelling. Responses, loyalties, and decisions can influence relationships and determine how certain missions unfold.

Phantom Liberty includes consequential story decisions and different narrative outcomes. Players interested in seeing the wider story can revisit important choices during later playthroughs.

### Side Activities and Exploration

Dogtown contains optional activities and discoveries beyond the main storyline. Exploring its streets and buildings can reveal additional encounters, characters, and rewards.

Side missions provide further context for the district and its residents, adding depth to the setting beyond the central espionage operation.

## Characters and Factions

### V

V remains the central playable character. Existing character attributes, equipment, cyberware, and combat abilities shape how the expansion can be experienced.

### Rosalind Myers

Rosalind Myers is the NUSA President whose arrival sets the main operation in motion. Her political position makes the events in Dogtown a matter of national importance.

### Solomon Reed

Reed is an intelligence agent drawn into a mission where professional obligations and personal loyalties are difficult to separate. His history becomes important as the operation develops.

### Songbird

Songbird is a netrunner with advanced technical abilities and a central role in the crisis. Her motives, circumstances, and relationships are key elements of the story.

### Kurt Hansen

Kurt Hansen controls Dogtown and maintains his authority through a powerful armed faction. His influence shapes the district's political and security landscape.

## World Design and Atmosphere

Phantom Liberty presents Dogtown as a distinct part of Night City, with crowded streets, damaged infrastructure, guarded compounds, and commercial areas operating under uncertain conditions.

Lighting, sound design, architecture, and environmental details help establish the district's tense atmosphere. Locations are designed to support both narrative scenes and varied mission encounters.

The expansion combines cinematic sequences with player-controlled investigation and combat. This balance keeps the story connected to the wider world while allowing players to influence how individual situations unfold.
